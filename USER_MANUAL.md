# HireNexa Enterprise Comprehensive User Manual

## End-to-End Operational Guide for Candidates, Recruiters, Company Admins & Platform Owners

---

## 1. Candidate & Student User Guide

### A. Account Setup & Profile Optimization
1. Navigate to `/register/` or `/candidate/login/`.
2. Fill in your name, email, target examination or career track (e.g., NIMCET MCA, OJEE MCA, Software Engineering).
3. Upload your resume in PDF/DOCX format in your candidate profile dashboard.
4. Access the **AI Career Optimization (ACO)** suite to analyze your resume against real job market requirements.

### B. Computer-Based Examination (CBT) Preparation
1. Navigate to `/exam-tracks/` or select a subject under MCA Entrance Preparation.
2. Choose a syllabus topic (e.g., *Data Structures*, *Calculus*, *Computer Architecture*).
3. Review the test parameters:
   - **Questions**: Exactly 16 questions
   - **Time Allowed**: 21 minutes (countdown timer displayed)
   - **Scoring**: +1.00 for correct, -0.25 for incorrect, 0.00 for unanswered
   - **Attempt Policy**: Exactly 5 attempts permitted per topic test
4. Click **Launch Topic Test**.
5. Practice with **Authentic 5-Year PYQs** (2020-2024 NIMCET, OJEE, CUET-PG) identified by the purple badge.
6. Submit the exam to receive your detailed scorecard, step-by-step mathematical derivations, concept notes, and common trap warnings.

### C. Job Discovery & Video Interview Participation
1. Explore verified jobs at `/jobs/` with GeoHire location filtering.
2. Submit your application with one click.
3. When invited to an interview by a recruiter, navigate to your dashboard or click the interview link.
4. Click **Enter Virtual Interview Room** (`/interviews/<pk>/room/`) to join the integrated studio or open the Zoom meeting link.

---

## 2. HR Recruiter User Guide

### A. Vanity Recruiter Access
1. Access the dedicated gateway at `/hr/login/`.
2. View your organization's recruitment dashboard with applicant pipelines, upcoming interviews, and open postings.

### B. Posting & Managing Jobs
1. Click **Post a New Job**.
2. Define job requirements, salary range, experience level, and GeoHire office location.
3. Publish the listing to instantly appear in the public job directory.

### C. Video Interview Scheduling & Conducting
1. Open an applicant's profile and click **Schedule Interview**.
2. Select your provider:
   - **HireNexa Internal Studio**: Zero third-party downloads, browser-based secure video room.
   - **Zoom**: Automatic Zoom meeting ID, password, and join link generation.
   - **Google Meet**: Meeting URL integration.
3. Set the date, time, and duration (e.g., 30, 45, or 60 minutes).
4. Launch the room at interview time (`/interviews/<pk>/room/`) to evaluate the candidate while simultaneously recording notes and scorecards.

---

## 3. Organization / Company Administrator Guide

### A. Corporate Office & GeoHire Verification
1. Log in at `/company/login/`.
2. Navigate to **Company Profile & Trust Settings**.
3. Enter your **Statutory Registered Office Address** (MCA/RoC filing).
4. Enter your **Branch / Development Center Office Address**.
5. Provide verified Google Maps links or turn-by-turn navigation URLs.
6. Save to achieve **Tier-A Verified Trust Status** on your public company profile (`/companies/<slug>/trust/`).

### B. Team Management
1. Invite HR recruiters to your company workspace.
2. Assign hiring permissions and job ownership.

---

## 4. Platform Owner / Administrator Guide

### A. System Administration Access
1. Access the master control center at `/owner/login/` or `/admin/`.
2. Monitor system health, user registration volume, and active test sessions.

### B. Curriculum & Question Bank Governance
1. Review question submissions from academic experts.
2. Inspect derivation steps, concept notes, and trap warnings.
3. Change status from `DRAFT` to `VERIFIED` and `PUBLISHED`.
