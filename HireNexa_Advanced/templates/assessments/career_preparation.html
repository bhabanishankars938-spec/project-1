🤝 Technical & HR Interview Master Framework

Behavioral storytelling frameworks and technical design methodologies.

⭐ The STAR Method for Behavioral & HR Questions

Use this structured framework to answer questions like "Tell me about a time you resolved a difficult conflict in a team":

S
Situation: Set the context and describe the specific scenario or challenge.
T
Task: Explain what your exact responsibility was in addressing the situation.
A
Action: Detail the specific steps and technical decisions YOU took.
R
Result: Share the quantifiable outcome, metrics, and what you learned.

💡 Top High-Frequency Technical Interview Questions

  • Explain ACID Properties in DBMS: Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability with real-world banking transaction example.
  • Process vs Thread: Process is an independent execution unit with isolated memory; Thread is a lightweight sub-process sharing the same process address space.
  • Virtual DOM Reconciliation: React computes a lightweight tree diff in JavaScript memory, avoiding expensive real browser DOM reflows and repaints.
  • TCP vs UDP: TCP is connection-oriented, reliable, with acknowledgements; UDP is connectionless, fast, best for live streaming and gaming.
