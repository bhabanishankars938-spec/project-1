DATA SECURITY & PRIVACY

HireNexa Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · Committed to Candidate Confidentiality and Enterprise Data Protection

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to deliver talent intelligence services:

  • Identity Data: Full Name, Email Address, and Verified Mobile Number.
  • Professional & Education Data: University, Degree, Graduation Year, Skills, Projects, and Work Experience.
  • Document Data: Uploaded PDF/DOCX resumes stored in encrypted private storage.
  • Security & Telemetry Data: IP addresses and user agents recorded in audit trails for fraud prevention.

2. How We Protect Your Documents

Uploaded resumes are kept in private server directories (`media/private_resumes/`) and are never exposed publicly. Access is gated by multi-tenant RBAC permissions: only the candidate themselves, Platform Owners, and recruiters whose jobs the candidate has applied to can access the resume file.

3. Cryptographic OTP Verification

Verification OTPs sent via Email or SMS are generated with cryptographically secure random integers and hashed using HMAC-SHA256 prior to storage. Plaintext OTPs are never stored in databases, logs, or API responses.

4. Consent Management & Revocation

Candidates maintain complete control over their resume sharing through the one-click Consent Center in their portal. Revoking consent instantly prevents hiring organizations from accessing resume downloads.

5. Account Deletion & Data Purge

You may permanently delete your account at any time via the Candidate Dashboard settings. Upon deletion, personal details, resume files, and applications are purged from operational databases.
