HireNexa Terms of Service

1. Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account, accessing portals, or submitting information to HireNexa, you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms of Service. If you do not agree to these terms, you must not use the platform.

2. Account Types & Verification

HireNexa supports three primary user roles: (a) Job Seekers and Candidates, (b) Employers, HR Managers, and Company Admins, and (c) Freelance Contractors. Platform Owner status is reserved exclusively for platform administrators.

All accounts require valid dual Email and Mobile OTP verification. Creating fraudulent accounts, impersonating companies, or using temporary fake email inboxes is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate suspension.

3. AI Screening & Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards

HireNexa provides AI-driven skill matching and resume diagnostics. AI scores serve strictly as decision support tools. Hiring organizations agree that all candidate advancement, shortlisting, interviewing, and rejection decisions remain under human control.

4. Employer Requisitions & Accuracy

Hiring organizations agree to post only legitimate, verified employment or freelance openings. Multi-level marketing (MLM), pyramid schemes, or suspicious unpaid listings are removed, and the posting account will be terminated.

5. Candidate Privacy & Resume Sharing

Candidate resumes and contact details are accessible only to verified hiring organizations for jobs to which the candidate has actively applied, provided the candidate maintains active resume-sharing consent.

6. Termination & Deletion

Users may request complete account deletion at any time from their portal settings. Following account deletion, personal records and uploaded resume documents are purged in accordance with data privacy regulations.
